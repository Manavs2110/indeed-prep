# Ex. 5 — Partner drill (60 min)

Pick one: **WhatsApp**, **Dropbox**, **Uber**, **YouTube**, **Netflix**.

Your partner's job: every 10 minutes, add one requirement from this list (or invent one):

- 10x the traffic overnight
- Must work offline / on bad networks
- Data must stay in the EU for EU users
- One region goes down entirely
- Product wants a feature that needs strong consistency
- Cut infra cost by 40%
- Add an AI feature (summaries, recommendations, moderation)

Partner scoring sheet (1–5 each): clarified requirements · justified tech choices · tradeoffs said aloud · adapted cleanly to new requirements · production readiness.
