# Ex. 4 — Apply flow with resume upload (45 min)

- A job seeker uploads a resume (PDF/DOCX, up to 10 MB) and applies to a job.
- The employer sees applicants for each job in a dashboard and can change their status (new → reviewed → interviewing → rejected/hired).

<details><summary>Twist A</summary>

All uploads must be **virus-scanned** before an employer can download them.
</details>

<details><summary>Twist B</summary>

A user submits a **GDPR deletion request**. Everything about them must be gone within 30 days.
</details>

<details><summary>Twist C</summary>

A viral job gets **10,000 applications in one hour**. What breaks for the employer?
</details>
