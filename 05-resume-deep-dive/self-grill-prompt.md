# Self-grill prompt

Paste this into Claude/ChatGPT, followed by your filled-in `project-story.md`:

```
You are a skeptical senior engineer at Indeed running a "Resumé Deep Dive"
interview. Below is my project write-up. Ask me 10 hard follow-up questions,
ONE at a time. Wait for my answer each time. Push back when I'm vague, when I
say "we" without clarifying my own role, or when I give no numbers. Cover:
technical decisions and alternatives, scale, metrics, team dynamics, mistakes,
and how I used and verified AI tools. At the end, score me 1-5 on: clarity,
ownership, use of metrics, technical depth, self-awareness — with one
concrete improvement each.
```
